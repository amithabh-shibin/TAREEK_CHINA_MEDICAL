Hainan Sanya Medical-Elderly Care Complex

A destination where healthcare meets wellness

The supplied project material describes a medical and elderly-care complex of more than 70,000 sqm GFA, combining a rehabilitation hospital, wellness hotel and health-management formats.

Located in Sanya's Haitang Bay area, the project is positioned to combine medical care, health checks, rehabilitation, wellness and vacation-style recovery. This creates a more comfortable environment for patients and accompanying family members who may need a longer recovery journey.
