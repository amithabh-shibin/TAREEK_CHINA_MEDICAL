Where medical innovation meets island living

BOAO LECHENG / HAINAN, CHINA

Hainan Boao Lecheng International Medical Tourism Pilot Zone is China’s exclusive and world-class international medical pilot zone. As the permanent venue of the Boao Forum for Asia, it enjoys four exclusive policy advantages in medical access, clinical research, international medical cooperation and commercial operation.

The zone has introduced more than 500 kinds of globally innovative imported medicines and gathered 36 officially operated high-standard hospitals equipped with world-leading medical equipment. It provides customized high-end physical examinations, targeted interventions for chronic conditions such as diabetes, nephropathy and joint damage, as well as internationally standardized stem cell anti-aging programs. Uniquely, Boao Lecheng integrates advanced stem cell therapy with traditional Chinese medicine philosophy. Instead of merely relieving superficial symptoms, treatments focus on repairing root causes and restoring physical functions from the inside out. All regimens are science-based and personally customized according to individual health assessment, rather than adopting a one-size-fits-all model.

Perspective / 01

Chinese health philosophy emphasizes preventive healthcare. Regular physical examinations help detect hidden health risks before symptoms appear, making proactive health investment the wisest long-term wellness strategy. Supported by strict national supervision, China has built a full-cycle health management system combining early screening and precise cellular intervention. Stem cell technology, as a core part of modern preventive medicine, works alongside routine check-ups to repair organ functions, delay cellular aging and intervene in aging-related chronic diseases.

Perspective / 02

Studies have scientifically confirmed that stem cells are the human body’s innate core repair system, governing tissue regeneration, organ renewal and physical self-recovery. The body’s repair capability entirely depends on the volume of active stem cells circulating in the blood.

Perspective / 03

Human stem cell reserves decline sharply with age. By 30 to 35 years old, the human body retains only approximately 10% of the stem cell quantity present at birth. Once stem cell levels fall below the biological threshold, complete tissue repair can no longer be achieved. This is precisely why physical recovery, metabolism and immunity weaken remarkably after the age of 40.

Perspective / 04

Insufficient stem cells disrupt normal tissue renewal and physiological metabolism. Continuous cell loss and cumulative tissue damage cannot be fully compensated, leading to gradual organ dysfunction, declining vitality and visible aging. Essentially, all aging symptoms and age-related chronic diseases originate from the progressive depletion and functional failure of the body’s endogenous stem cell repair system.

Perspective / 05

Modern preventive stem cell therapy has shown outstanding clinical value, especially in cardiovascular protection. Clinical trials verify that stem cell intervention improves overall cardiac function. Most significantly, high-risk groups achieve a substantial reduction in the incidence of both myocardial infarction and stroke, delivering powerful root-level prevention for aging and degenerative diseases.

Perspective / 06

Stem cell anti-aging cannot achieve optimal results through a single treatment session. Consistent conditioning is essential. Generally, 3 to 4 treatment courses per year are required for stable and visible improvement. Since chronic degeneration develops over years, physical repair and anti-aging renewal rely on continuous, standardized medical intervention.

Perspective / 07

With the continuous advancement of global clinical research and standardized medical regulation, stem cell preventive therapy at Boao Lecheng continues to deliver scientific, safe and long-term solutions for deep anti-aging, organ function restoration and chronic disease intervention.

Stem Cell and Immune Cell Therapy

Stem cells and immune cells function via completely different mechanisms.

Stem cells focus on physical repair: they restore tissues and organs, regulate the internal microenvironment, and boost the body’s self-healing capacity.

Immune cells specialize in bodily defense: they carry out immune recognition and surveillance, balance immune functions, and strengthen the body’s resistance to harmful substances.

Neither type can replace the other, and neither is superior. First identify your core health troubles: weakened self-recovery, constant fatigue, or immune disorders. Conduct a comprehensive assessment based on physical examination indicators and track physical changes afterward. Choosing cell therapy is not an either-or decision. Treatments will be matched to your personal health conditions, and professionals will provide targeted advice according to your test reports.

Blood Purification

Degraded blood function is the core factor that accelerates human aging.

Scientifically, aging is not only manifested externally as sagging skin and wrinkles. Various aging biomarkers in blood can truly reflect the body’s aging condition. Excessive accumulation of free radicals, long-term low-grade inflammation and trapped metabolic waste will trigger premature aging symptoms such as dull complexion, sagging facial skin, slow metabolism and persistent fatigue, continuously raising the body’s biological age.

Blood purification is a professional medical technology. It extracts blood from the human body, filters and removes pathogenic substances through special purification equipment, and then transfuses the purified blood back into the body to prevent and treat diseases.

Blood purification deeply cleanses the blood and relieves physical burdens caused by oxidative buildup and metabolic stress. It accurately removes aging free radicals, metabolic toxins and inflammatory factors in the blood, dredges systemic circulation, improves fatigue and dull skin caused by long-term poor circulation, and fundamentally eases the aging burden brought by internal metabolism.
